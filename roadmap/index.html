Where we're going.

Norvia Labs is building one thing in stages: the controls that let a company put AI agents to work. It starts with one developer, grows to a team, and ends with every agent a company runs.

Get in touch See the platform

The bet

Agents are getting more capable every quarter, and each one ships its own permissions. What companies lack is one set of controls across all of them: what they can touch, who signs off, and proof of what they did. That layer outlasts any single model or agent, and it's what we're building.

Three stages.

Each stage ships something useful on its own, and each has a test it must pass before the next one starts.

  1. Prove the controls in one terminal.

    Stage 1, Developers SHIPPING

    Forge puts an OS sandbox and an ALLOW / ASK / DENY policy under a coding agent, as open source, so the hard parts get tested on real work in public.

    We move on when one Guard policy runs unchanged in Forge and in Conclave.

  2. Share the controls across a team.

    Stage 2, Teams BUILDING

    Conclave runs agents and people as one team with approvers built in. A shared policy engine and one approval queue make the controls a team asset, not a per-laptop setting.

    We move on when the gateway governs an agent we didn't build, end to end.

  3. Control every agent a company runs.

    Stage 3, Companies PLANNED

    The gateway sits in front of every agent, ours or not. Policy, approvals, audit and short-lived credentials are managed in one place, by the people accountable for them.

    Done looks like a security team answering "what did our agents do last week?" in one query.

What we won't build.

Focus is a decision about what to leave out.

  • Our own model.The controls work with whichever model you use. Forge already connects to OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and local Ollama models.
  • Another chat app.Agents already have interfaces. We sit underneath them, in the path of what they do.
  • Model routing as a product.Others do it well. We route only as far as policy needs.
  • Rules that live in a prompt.If a model can talk its way past a control, it isn't one. Everything we ship is enforced below the agent.

We build tools for putting AI agents to work.

Norvia Labs was founded in October 2025 by Mohit Ranka. We make AI agents useful in real work, in the codebase and across a team, without giving up control or the ability to check what they did.

Why now

AI agents have become active participants in software development and operations. They run commands, call APIs, and change systems that matter. The tooling underneath them hasn't caught up on execution, permissions, observability, contracts, provenance and human oversight.

The agentic era isn't short on demos. It's short on infrastructure that survives contact with production: governance that actually governs, identities that expire, loops that stop, contracts for the systems agents call, and audits you can prove.

That's the line through everything we make. Forge confines coding agents with the operating system. Conclave runs teams of agents and people where anything that changes the world waits for an approver. Next comes the control layer: one gateway that brings those habits to every agent a company runs, whoever built it. The roadmap sets out the stages.

How we work

  • Enforcement over instructions.Controls live in the OS, the network and the platform, where a model can't argue with them.
  • Open where it counts.Forge is open source under MIT, with source, issues and design notes public.
  • Prove it on real work first.Every part of the platform is proven on real agent sessions, in Forge or Conclave, before it becomes a product of its own.
  • Honest status.What's shipped and what's in development are always labelled as such.

Founder

Norvia Labs was founded by Mohit Ranka, who has spent 18 years building the platforms other teams depend on. At Booking.com he built identity and access management for 300M+ guests and 10M+ partners. At Postman he led the Runtime team that took Postman to the browser and beyond HTTP. At LinkedIn he led two teams that moved 1,500 sales datasets onto one data platform. At Moveworks he led analytics and real-time data integration for an agentic AI assistant used at more than 350 enterprises.

He wrote Forge himself, in the open. Norvia Labs is the infrastructure he wanted while doing that work: agents that can act, inside limits a person sets.
